Hardware · The Return of Touch

Apple Unveils Touchscreen MacBook Pro With ProLED XDR Display

After years of saying it would never happen, Apple brings touch to the Mac with tandem OLED, Dynamic Island, and M5 Pro/Max chips.
$2,899 14" starting price

Apple has unveiled its first touchscreen MacBook Pro, featuring a tandem OLED ProLED XDR display, a light-up logo, Dynamic Island, and either M5 Pro or M5 Max chips. The 14" model starts at $2,899 and the 16" at $3,399. Apple says it redesigned the hinge for stability and added an enhanced six-speaker system.

For years, Apple executives insisted the Mac was “first and foremost” built around keyboard and trackpad. But the company now says sometimes “it's comfortable and handy to have touch”. The new interface adapts to context, and Dynamic Island brings iPhone-like notifications and controls to the Mac for the first time.

It's a significant shift in philosophy for Apple, but one that feels inevitable as touch has become ubiquitous. The question isn't whether touch belongs on Mac — it's whether Apple can make it feel like a natural extension rather than a grudging concession.

02 · EARNINGS
Big Tech · Diverging Fortunes

Alphabet Misses as AI Mode Slows Search Growth, Microsoft Beats

GOOG drops 9%+ on weak search monetization, while Microsoft posts strong Azure growth and $10B in OpenAI revenue.
$10B OpenAI revenue for Microsoft

Alphabet reported Q3 revenue of $127.58B (up 25% YoY) but below expectations, with Search revenue growth slowing to 12% from 14.5% last year as AI Mode shows lower monetization. Google Cloud grew 103% to $30.7B, but overall the miss sent shares down over 9%. YouTube ad revenue grew just 9% to $11.2B.

Microsoft beat on both top and bottom lines with Q1 revenue of $92.28B (up 19%), Azure revenue up 48% to $33.17B, and net income up 32% to $36.63B. The company recorded $10B in revenue from OpenAI (nearly 11% of total), and capex came in at $45.2B.

The contrast is telling: investors are rewarding infrastructure providers tied to AI spending, but punishing those seeing AI cannibalize their core ad business. The AI transition is creating winners and losers even among Big Tech incumbents.

Models · Security-Focused AI

Google Unveils Gemini 4 Carbon, Its Strongest Cybersecurity Model

Rolling out to select defenders via Fairwind as the AI arms race extends to security capabilities.
#1 on Intelligence Index

Google unveiled Gemini 4 Carbon, calling it its “strongest model with state-of-the-art cybersecurity” capabilities and ranking #1 on Artificial Analysis’ updated Intelligence Index. It's being rolled out to selected cybersecurity defenders through Fairwind at $2/1M input and $10/1M output tokens, rising to $4 and $20 later.

Meanwhile, GitHub announced Copilot Fusion is now generally available, using intelligent model orchestration to pick the best model for each task. GitHub says it's seeing 7.4B commits per month (up from 2.9B in August) and has added 4M CPU cores and 160PB of storage in 2026 to support growth.

AI labs are increasingly specializing models for specific domains like cybersecurity, where the stakes are high and demand from defenders is growing. The next phase of AI competition may be won by models optimized for vertical use cases, not just general capability.

04 · SECURITY
Data Breach · Privacy

Massive Leak Exposes 41,000+ DHS Employees Including Undercover Agents

Hackers breached DHS’ Okta login system, leaking CBP, ICE and other agency workers’ personal data.
41K+ employees exposed

Hackers have leaked the personal data of 41,000+ DHS employees and contractors, including those working for CBP and ICE. The leaked records include workers’ cell phone numbers, locations of ICE and CBP facilities, and members of groups tied to undercover operations. DDoSecrets says the data came from DHS’ Okta login system.

Bluesky has blocked links to the archive, saying the data was “shared without their authorization”. The breach is particularly alarming given it exposes undercover agents and sensitive facility locations, potentially putting lives at risk.

Government agencies handling sensitive immigration and border enforcement are prime targets. This breach underscores the catastrophic consequences of even a single compromised identity provider.

Memory · Deals

Samsung Hits Record Profits as HBM Sells Out Through 2027

Memory prices surge as AI demand overwhelms supply, while Marvell eyes $83B Lumentum acquisition.
$63B Samsung capex in 2026

Samsung Electronics reported record Q3 results: revenue up 127% YoY to ~$146.3B, operating profit up 783% to ~$80.5B. The chip unit alone generated ~$110B in revenue. Samsung says HBM capacity is sold out through 2027 and it has secured 10 long-term supply deals with key customers. Capex is expected to rise ~60% to ~$63B in 2026.

Meanwhile, Marvell is in advanced talks to buy data center optics maker Lumentum in a deal that could be worth tens of billions. The DOJ is also inquiring about Nvidia’s Hugging Face deal over competition concerns.

The memory crunch is reshaping the entire tech stack. When HBM capacity is sold out two years out, it tells you everything about how insatiable AI compute demand really is.
